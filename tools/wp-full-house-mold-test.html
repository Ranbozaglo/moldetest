EPA & CDC Moisture Prevention Guidelines

Achieving lasting indoor air quality requires pairing accurate testing with strict moisture management.

Follow EPA and CDC Indoor Blueprint:

  • Keep indoor relative humidity strictly between 30% and 50% using dehumidification and kitchen/bath exhaust fans.
  • Dry any active leaks, spills, or flooding within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold growth from taking root.
  • Do not scrape, bleach, or wipe visible mold yourself. Disturbing growth can release spores into the air. Share your lab report with a qualified mold remediation professional.

See guidance from the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) and the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC).
