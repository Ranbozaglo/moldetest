EPA & CDC Indoor Moisture Standards

Testing provides accurate surface data, but stopping mold long-term requires controlling indoor humidity and water leaks.

Follow Official EPA and CDC Guidelines:

  • Maintain indoor relative humidity between 30% and 50% using air conditioning, dehumidifiers, and exhaust fans.
  • Dry any active leaks, spills, or condensation within 24 to 48 hours to prevent fungal spore germination.
  • Do not scrape, bleach, or wipe visible mold yourself. Disturbing growth can release spores into the air. Share your lab report with a qualified mold remediation professional.

See guidance from the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) and the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC).
