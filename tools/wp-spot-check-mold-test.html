Moisture Control & EPA Guidance

Testing identifies what is growing on your sampled surfaces, but preventing future growth requires controlling moisture.

According to guidelines from the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) and the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC):

  • Maintain relative indoor humidity between 30% and 50% using dehumidifiers and exhaust ventilation.
  • Clean and dry any plumbing leaks, spills, or water intrusion within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold spores from taking root.
  • Do not scrape, bleach, or wipe visible mold yourself. Disturbing growth can release spores into the air. Share your lab report with a qualified mold remediation professional.
