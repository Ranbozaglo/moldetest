Quick takeaways

  • Humidity plus cold AC surfaces is a common reason vents and bathrooms stain in Gulf Coast homes.
  • Swab the stain if you need a lab ID or a written record. Do not scrape or wipe the growth.
  • A TDLR on-site assessment is a different product from a mail-in swab.
  • Stop leaks and dry standing water within 24–48 hours if you can do that without touching the colony.

When a mail-in swab helps

  • You can see a stain on grout, a vanity, a window sill, or an HVAC grille.
  • You need a species ID before you call a remediator, or a record for a landlord or buyer.
  • You want several rooms sampled without scheduling a visit. Full House is six samples; Extended is four; Spot Check is two.

How to sample without a kit: household cotton swab + prepaid label. Related: musty smell, nothing visible and DIY vs professional inspection.

When you need an on-site licensed assessment

Texas regulates mold assessment and remediation. If a lender, insurer, or local rule requires a licensed consultant to inspect the property, a mail-in swab will not replace that walkthrough. Hidden moisture behind finished walls, sewage, or a large contaminated area is also outside what a household swab can document. Hire the licensed professional for that work. Do not treat a lab ID of one grille as a whole-house clearance.

Frequently asked questions

Does Houston humidity cause mold?

Mold needs moisture. Gulf humidity and AC coils keep indoor surfaces damp. A swab still only names the stain you sample.

Is a mail-in swab the same as a Houston mold inspection?

No. Mail-in = inspector-reviewed lab analysis of your swabs. On-site = a licensed assessor at the property.

Can I swab HVAC vents in a Houston home?

Yes if you can reach a stained grille. Turn the system off. The result is that grille. More: mold or dirt around vents.
